# Petrichor

A cozy rainy room in your browser. Rain streaks down a foggy window over a night city, and everything on the sill is clickable.

- **Foggy glass**: wipe it with your cursor; fog slowly grows back. Raindrops refract the city lights.
- **Real clock**: the sky, lights and room follow your local time (use the time slider to preview).
- **Sound mixer** (all generated with the Web Audio API, no audio files): rain that muffles as your cursor leaves the window, radio with generative lofi and vinyl crackle, ticking clock, whistling kettle, purring cat, thunder.
- **Journal**: write a thought and watch it appear on the glass, then evaporate.
- **Storm mode**: lightning, thunder, and a startled cat.

- **Weather modes**: rain, storm (lightning bolts + thunder), clear (sun, stars, birds), cold (frost, snow, wind).
- **Tame the cat**: stroke it slowly; it warms up and gets a collar.
- **Sill and desk**: tilted phone with the real time, water the plant until it blooms, brew coffee, light the candle.
- **Radio**: search any song and play a 30-second preview, kept low under the rain (iTunes Search API, no key needed).

## Themes

Pick a room when it opens (or use *switch theme* any time):

- **Rain**: the foggy window room above.
- **Spring**: open window with swaying curtains, blossom trees and falling petals, fireflies at night, string lights, a mushroom lamp and a paper lantern you can light, and Biscuit the puppy you can train (sit, shake, roll over). The sky, sun, moon and sounds follow your real local time.

## Run

Open `index.html` in a browser (or use the VS Code Live Server extension).
